HanClip usage check:
- Commercial use: allowed.
- App/software bundling and redistribution: allowed when the copyright notice
  and license are included.
- Embedding with software: allowed under the SIL OFL terms.
- User-created video output: allowed; documents or media created with the font
  are not required to be licensed under the font license.
- Modification/redistribution: allowed under SIL OFL terms, but Reserved Font
  Names may not be used for modified versions without permission.
- Copyright notice: include this notice and the license when redistributing
  the font with the app.
