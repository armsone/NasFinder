License / usage statement found:
- The official Puradak page provides the font as a free download.
- The usage guide allows commercial and non-commercial use, video subtitles,
  app UI, and bundling/redistribution with other software when the full
  copyright and license notice is displayed.

HanClip usage check:
- Commercial use: allowed.
- App/software bundling and redistribution: allowed with copyright/license
  notice.
- User-created video output: allowed, including video subtitles.
- Modification: allowed by the usage guide, but HanClip bundles the unmodified
  official TTF file.
- Paid sale of the font file by itself: prohibited.
- Copyright notice: include this notice with the app bundle.
